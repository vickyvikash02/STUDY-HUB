Questions

Frequently asked

How do mock tests work?

Open Mock Tests, pick a paper and start. You get a countdown timer, a question palette, mark-for-review and clear-response controls. Submit any time to see your score and a full review. Tests are created in the Test Builder.

Can I create my own test?

Yes. In Test Builder, filter the question bank by category, subcategory and topic, tick the questions you want, set a name, duration and marks, then save. Your test appears instantly under Mock Tests.

Where do the questions come from?

Your library is fully editable. Add categories, subcategories, topics and questions — with optional images and explanations — from the Admin panel.

How do I download e-books?

Open the E-Book Library and hit View on any title. Admins can add PDFs by pasting a Google Drive link or by linking a shared Drive folder.

Is my progress saved?

Everything is saved to your browser's local storage on this device, so your questions, tests and e-books stay available without signing in. Clearing browser data will remove them.
